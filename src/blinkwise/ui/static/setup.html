Before the camera turns on

  • Everything stays on this computer. Camera images are analysed in memory and discarded. They are never saved or sent anywhere.
  • Not a medical device. Blinkwise estimates habits with a webcam to help you build better ones. It doesn't diagnose or treat anything, and its numbers can be wrong.
  • See an eye-care professional for pain, redness, persistent dryness, blurred vision or any change in your sight.
  • Never use it for safety decisions, such as whether you're alert enough to drive.
