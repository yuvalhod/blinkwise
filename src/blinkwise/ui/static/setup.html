Set up Blinkwise

Two minutes to make sure Blinkwise can see your eyes well. Nothing here is recorded, only checked.

1. Sit as you normally do

Look at the middle of the screen you use most. Blinkwise works best with the camera roughly in front of you.

    2. Blink 3 times

    Normal, relaxed blinks. Each one ticks off below (there's about a second of delay).

    Waiting for blinks...

    3. How present should Blinkwise be?

    You can change this any time from the menu bar.

    You're set

    Blinkwise is now in your menu bar (the closed eye). It works best when:

    • the camera faces you, within about 20 degrees of where you look (on top of your main screen is ideal);
    • your face is lit from the front or side, not by a bright window behind you;
    • you're about an arm's length (50-70 cm) away;
    • no sunglasses (normal glasses are fine; tilt them slightly if reflections hide your eyes).
